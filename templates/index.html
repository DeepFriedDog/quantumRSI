Quantum Probability Model

This advanced strategy applies quantum probability theory to asset returns:

  • Multiple energy levels create multimodal return distributions
  • Active Trading Intentions (ATI) represented through complex-valued probability amplitudes
  • Conservative, Balanced, and Aggressive parameter sets target different risk profiles
Quantum Stock Market Model (Zhang and Huang)
  • Treats stock prices as quantum particles within an infinite potential well
  • Incorporates an external field to represent market information
  • Uses the Schrödinger equation to model price evolution over time
  • Predicts multi-modal return distributions based on field influence
Quantum Oscillator Model of Stock Markets (David Orrell)
  • Models markets as quantum harmonic oscillators with discrete energy levels
  • Each energy level corresponds to an integer number of transactions
  • Derived by quantizing entropic forces representing buying/selling intentions
  • Explains the non-Gaussian nature of returns and the square-root law of price impact
  • Relates volatility to market spread through a quantum uncertainty framework
Quantum Anharmonic Oscillator Model
  • Extends the quantum oscillator with cubic (β) and quartic (γ) potential terms
  • Models asymmetric return distributions with controlled skewness and kurtosis
  • Captures market asymmetry between buying and selling pressure
  • Explains "volatility smile" and fat-tailed distributions in financial markets
  • Adapts to changing market regimes by estimating the quantum state from recent data
Quantum Tunneling of Stock Price (Ovidiu Racorean)
  • Models explosive breakouts from range-bound markets as quantum tunneling events
  • Uses financial time-independent Schrödinger equation to calculate tunneling probability
  • Identifies that volatility drops dramatically before tunneling/breakout events
  • Provides transmission coefficient to quantify probability of price penetrating support/resistance
  • Mathematically explains why price can "tunnel through" barriers that should contain it
